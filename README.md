# 📐 Our Remodeling Process

### 01 — Consultation

We discuss your ideas, priorities, remodeling goals, preferred style, and project requirements.

### 02 — Property Evaluation

The project area is reviewed to understand the work required and identify possible improvements.

### 03 — Planning

We define the project scope, materials, design direction, and renovation requirements.

### 04 — Preparation

The workspace is prepared before construction and remodeling begins.

### 05 — Renovation

Our team completes the agreed remodeling work with attention to quality and detail.

### 06 — Quality Review

The completed work is carefully reviewed before final project delivery.

### 07 — Project Completion

The renovated space is delivered ready for the homeowner to enjoy.
